# Overgrown Shrine: Moving Door and Eye Statue Help

Two different mistakes can stop progress here: treating a moved door as if it must be opened in its original position, and copying an eye statue without checking its facing direction. Identify which situation matches your room before changing the layout again.

## The 2F lever and moving door

For the second-floor lever room described in the Steam discussion, the developer gives this order:

1. Find the closed door to the left of the lever.
2. Pull the lever so the floor shifts and carries the door to a new position.
3. Open the door while it is in that new position.
4. Restore the moving floor to its original position, then check the changed route.

The important distinction is between moving the room and opening the door. Merely toggling the lever back and forth omits the door interaction between those two changes. [Developer room hint](https://steamcommunity.com/app/3100970/discussions/0/583933098654442513/#c583933098654442843)

This instruction applies to the room in that discussion. It does not prescribe a universal lever order for the whole dungeon.

## The eye statue copy

Check which direction the eye faces **at the moment you copy it**. The developer explicitly identifies this as relevant. The player subsequently reports solving the puzzle and explains that an unreachable second statue had been a distraction. Recheck the available source before assuming that distant statue is required. [Developer answer and follow-up](https://steamcommunity.com/app/3100970/discussions/0/583933098654442513/#c583933098654443350)

The discussion does not establish one direction or placement that works for every eye-statue room. Use the facing direction at the source as the thing to change when retrying this specific puzzle.
